# The season, as a coach sees it

Generated from the plan engine with the starting config. When you change a race date, the app rebuilds all of this.

## Phases
| Phase | Weeks | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation: the 10 km | 1 to 6 | Consistent running habits, run/walk efficiency, basic strength and swim endurance, ready for the 10 km. |
| Endurance: the half marathon | 7 to 15 | Long run grows step by step with easier weeks, aerobic base, muscular endurance and a longer non-stop swim. |
| Ramadan: maintain and recover | 16 to 19 | One light session a day around suhoor and iftar: keep the habit, keep strength, recover from the half marathon. No hard training while dry-fasting. |
| Run and strength base | 20 to 20 | Running and strength while the bike has not started yet. |
| Triathlon development | 21 to 25 | Cycling comes in after Ramadan: bike handling, cadence and easy endurance, first brick sessions, 1.5 km swims, strength still a priority. |
| HYROX preparation | 26 to 33 | Race-specific HYROX work: stations, running under fatigue, sled-style pushing and pulling, grip and wall-ball technique. |
| Olympic triathlon tune-up | 34 to 37 | Swim, bike and run together at Olympic distance, plus race nutrition practice. |
| Ironman 70.3 preparation | 38 to 43 | Long rides towards 90 km, long swims, long runs off the bike, race-day fuelling practice, then a two-week taper. Strength stays, at lower volume. |

## Weekly structure
| Day | Run phases (to Feb) | Triathlon and 70.3 phases | HYROX phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Fasting: walk and mobility after 18:50 | same | same |
| Tue | AM quality run, PM strength: legs, hamstrings, glutes | same | same |
| Wed | AM strength: shoulders, arms, back, PM swim: technique and intervals | same | same |
| Thu | Fasting: walk and mobility | same | same |
| Fri | AM easy run, PM light core, hips and arms | AM bike intervals, PM strength: hamstrings, shoulders, arms | AM bike intervals, PM HYROX stations |
| Sat | AM long run | AM brick (bike then run) or long run, alternating | AM HYROX simulation or long run, alternating |
| Sun | AM rest walk, PM non-stop swim | AM long ride, PM non-stop swim | AM long ride, PM non-stop swim |

During Ramadan, each day has one light session: a short run before suhoor (Tue, Sat), strength after iftar (Wed), and swims after iftar (Fri, Sun). Monday and Thursday stay walk and mobility only.

## Coach's notes
- **10 km on 5 Dec.**
  - Your Garmin runs average about 17 min per km with walking, and under 2 hours needs 12:00 per km.
  - The long run grows carefully from your real 3.4 km (4.5, then 6 km) instead of jumping to 7 to 8.5 km.
  - Race it as run-walk. A 1:45 finish is a realistic spring target once your easy pace improves.
- **Half marathon on 7 Feb.**
  - The long run builds 9.5, 11, 13 km, an easier 10 km, then 15 and 17 km, with a 2-week taper.
  - The 17 km run includes race-day food and water practice.
- **Ramadan.** It is expected to start around 7 or 8 Feb.
  - If it starts on race day, decide in advance whether you will fast that day. The app has a setting for the real dates once the moon is sighted.
  - Training during Ramadan is maintenance only.
- **1,500 m swim test.** Moved to Sun 14 Mar, the first Sunday after Ramadan.
- **Cycling.** Starts Mon 15 Mar.
  - You need a bike that fits you by then (see the Cycling checklist in the app).
  - The long ride builds to about 3 h 15 in late July. That is enough to finish a 90 km 70.3 bike, not to race it fast.
- **HYROX in June.** The date is a placeholder until you book.
  - From late April, try to get to a gym with a SkiErg, rower and sled once a week.
  - Every station also has a home version.
- **70.3 block.** It is only 6 weeks between the Olympic tune-up and the 70.3.
  - If you can choose, a later-August or September 70.3 gives a longer build.
  - Changing the date in Profile > Races rebuilds the plan around it.
- **35 km run.** Not scheduled. It would cost 2 to 3 weeks of recovery in the middle of the bike build. Set a date after the 70.3.
- **Fasting.** You dry-fast on Monday and Thursday, so those days have no demanding training.
  - The coach also treats any day you mark as fasting this way.
  - Never dry-fast on a race day.
